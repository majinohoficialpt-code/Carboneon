GOOD TO KNOW

Does it really double my storage?

No physical drive can be doubled by a webpage. This UI treats that as a playful “expanded capacity” feature. Real storage expansion requires additional disk space, compression, cloud storage, or similar technology.

Can the site auto-run the ZIP as admin?

Not safely from a normal browser session. Downloading the ZIP works; extracting and launching software as administrator requires a local, user-approved step.

Can I use my own MediaFire file?

Yes. Replace the placeholder URL in assets/app.js and the button will point to your file.
